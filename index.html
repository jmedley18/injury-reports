Live NFL, MLB & NBA injury reports

Sideline Status is a free web app that shows the latest injury report for every NFL, MLB and NBA team: who is Out, Doubtful, Questionable, Day-to-Day or on the Injured List, with the injury, an estimated return date and the latest note. Track your fantasy players with My Players (search, paste a list or import a Sleeper roster), get alerts when a status changes, and check live scores, upcoming games and where to watch.

Data comes from ESPN's public sports feeds and is loaded live. Not affiliated with ESPN, the NFL, MLB or the NBA. Add it to your home screen for an app-like experience.
